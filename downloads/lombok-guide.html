The Lombok Guide

The three projects that passed the first test, and how you would actually get into each one.

Companion to Report 01, One Month on Lombok · July 2026

This is the part I keep off the public page. In the report I wrote about what a month on the ground actually showed me: some construction in poor shape, sea views that may not survive the next project next door, and thousands of near-identical villas going up at once. Of roughly ten projects I looked at and did a basic level of diligence on, three passed the first test.

Those three gave me clean, transparent answers and the supporting documentation to back them up. Below is each one, what it is, and the entry point and structure as it stands today.

How to read this. These are the terms as they stand right now, and they move. Minimums change, allocations fill up, and structures get adjusted. Nothing here is a recommendation or an offer, and none of it is tax or legal advice. Before committing to anything, verify the current terms directly and take your own professional advice for your situation.

The three projects

In the same order as the report.

1

The Green Hub

Lionel and Javi · Kuta, South Lombok

The first dedicated coworking space in Kuta with world-class facilities and on-site accommodation. Coworking combined with co-living, plus a cafe for hospitality revenue, an auditorium to rent out for events, and a flexible layout that can shift to more or fewer private offices as demand moves. The roof is going on now, and they expect to be operational within six months.

Minimum investment
$30K, increasing to $40K soon
Structure
Singaporean SPV. Ownership share is given out, and returns are paid out through the SPV.
Stage
Under construction, roof going on. Operational target within six months.
Why it stood out
A real concept rather than another villa. Multiple revenue lines, and it matches where remote work is actually going.
2

Canvas

Shaun and Brandon · Are Guling, South Lombok

A multi-faceted space on top of a hill overlooking Areguling beach. At its base a boutique hotel with beautiful finishes and views, but built with company retreats and small congresses in mind, which fits Shaun's contacts in the corporate space and the growing demand for places to take teams for team-building, product launches or client events, combined with a program of exploring Lombok and surfing.

Minimum investment
$100K
Structure
Singapore SPV.
Why it stood out
Transparency and documentation. Shaun has construction and real estate experience, built four villas himself while documenting the whole thing, and is selective about who he works with.
3

Tampah Beach Villas

Baraca Development · Tampah beach, South Lombok

Right on Tampah beach, probably the most exclusive part of Southern Lombok. Villas, most with private pools or sitting on a shared lagoon. In terms of location, finishes and concept it is hard to beat, and a known Dutch contractor has been hired to supervise the project.

Entry level
€220K
Returns
Positive expected returns. Current figures on request.
Why it stood out
I worked at Baraca long enough to see how things are done there. Diana oversees this one, and she is my go-to person because I can always expect an honest answer.

A fourth one is in validation
